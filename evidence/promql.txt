A Counter would be wrong for in-flight requests because in-flight requests can increase and decrease; a Counter can only increase, while a Gauge can move up and down.

Query 1 — Request rate over 5 minutes by endpoint:
sum by (endpoint) (rate(capstone_requests_total[5m]))
